Dawurogna Figurative Speaking is an offline-first Flutter app preserving Dawuro proverbs and figurative expressions, with phonetic transcriptions and Amharic translations. Published on Google Play as Dawuro Proverbs.

The Challenge

Dawuro oral traditions need an accessible digital home. Speakers and language learners need a way to explore traditional sayings, understand their pronunciation and meaning, and revisit them even when internet access is unavailable.

Architecture

A bundled JSON collection feeds a local repository indexed by proverb ID and alphabet category. Provider controllers manage browsing and favorites, Hive persists saved proverb IDs on-device, and shared preferences retain theme settings. GoRouter connects the screens; core reading and favorites work without a server or network connection.

Solution & Impact

Readers browse by alphabet, learn through Amharic translations, save favorites, share sayings, and discover a daily proverb in light or dark mode. The production Android app makes Dawuro cultural knowledge available offline to speakers, students, and anyone exploring the language.

An internal business operations system for Barkot Bar & Restaurant in Addis Ababa, bringing point of sale, inventory, finance, and monthly payroll into one role-controlled web application.

The Challenge

Running a restaurant means reconciling every sale, bottle, expense, and payslip. The system needs to keep those records consistent, prevent duplicate sales when requests are retried, and separate staff duties so the person preparing a payment cannot also approve it.

Architecture

A Next.js 16 and TypeScript frontend calls a versioned Django 5.2 and DRF API, with PostgreSQL as the single source of truth. Session authentication, CSRF protection, and backend-enforced permissions serve four roles. Atomic, idempotent operations write an append-only stock ledger and audit trail; decimal money fields preserve financial precision. Docker Compose packages the stack, with Nginx handling production routing and TLS.

Solution & Impact

Staff record sales, receive and count stock, approve expenses, and run monthly payroll through dedicated workflows. Owners get derived financial balances and downloadable PDF reports, while sensitive actions retain an audit history. Backend, frontend, and browser test suites support the release pipeline. The production stack has been rehearsed locally; VPS deployment awaits client decisions.

A field maintenance reporting app for technicians at remote satellite sites, built to turn a maintenance issue into a digital report that reaches every technician and manager on the job instantly.

The Challenge

Field technicians logged maintenance issues on paper or disconnected spreadsheets. Reports were slow to file, easy to lose, and impossible to track once submitted — especially in areas with unreliable signal.

Architecture

A Flutter app for iOS and Android with a local-first data layer: reports are captured and queued on-device, then synced automatically the moment connectivity returns. Predictable state management keeps the UI consistent through connectivity drops, with structured photo capture and role-based workflows for field agents vs. supervisors.

Solution & Impact

Technicians file a complete report in seconds, with or without signal. Supervisors get a live queue instead of a paper backlog. The result: faster repair turnaround, an auditable history of every report, and effectively zero lost submissions.

A retrieval-augmented legal assistant that answers questions on Ethiopian law — grounding every response in real legal documents and citing its sources, instead of relying on generic model recall.

The Challenge

Ethiopian legal information is scattered across sources with no central place to search it — and even when you find the right document, it rarely comes with an explanation. Lawyers lose hours preparing for court cases, researchers struggle to locate the right material, and everyday citizens have no easy way to understand what the law actually means for them.

Architecture

Django and Django REST Framework power an ingestion pipeline that indexes Ethiopian legal texts — codes, proclamations, and case materials — for retrieval. When a lawyer, student, or citizen asks a question, the system pulls the relevant passages first and passes them to the Mistral API to generate a plain-language answer with the source cited, so nothing is stated without a document behind it. A React frontend delivers the query/response experience, with caching and rate-limit handling to keep answers fast under load.

Solution & Impact

Instead of digging through scattered legal texts, users ask a question in plain language and get an answer grounded in the actual law — with a citation attached. Lawyers cut down research and case-prep time, students and researchers find relevant material in seconds, and everyday citizens get a plain-language explanation of laws that affect them.
